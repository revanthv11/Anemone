# Anemone end user license agreement (draft)

*A draft for the custom EULA field in App Store Connect (App Information › License Agreement), so the App Store listing points to the GPL instead of Apple's standard EULA ("GPL and the App Store" in `docs/PLAN.md`). It follows Apple's minimum terms for a custom EULA. It isn't legal advice: the wording is the developer's decision, ideally with a lawyer's review. Replace the bracketed parts.*
