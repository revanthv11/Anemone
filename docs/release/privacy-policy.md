Anemone is a client for Jellyfin media servers on Apple TV. It's made by [developer name] and is free software under the GNU General Public License, version 3.

## What Anemone collects

Nothing. The developer doesn't run any server for Anemone and receives no data from it: no analytics, no advertising, no crash reports and no tracking.

## What Anemone sends, and where

Anemone talks only to the Jellyfin servers you add. To work, it sends them:

- your user name and password when you sign in, in exchange for an access token that stays in your Apple TV's Keychain;
- a device name and a random device ID, so the server can tell your sessions apart;
- requests for your libraries, artwork and videos;
- what you play and how far you got, so the server can resume and mark things watched;
- settings you choose to save on the server, such as your home rows.

Your Jellyfin server is run by you or by whoever gave you access to it; its own privacy practices are up to its owner.

When you play a trailer that's hosted on YouTube, Anemone opens the YouTube app, which has its own privacy policy.

## What stays on your Apple TV

Your saved servers and profiles, your Anemone settings and a cache of artwork stay on the device. Removing Anemone removes them.
